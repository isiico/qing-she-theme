# 《青蛇》主题 / Green Snake Theme

**我在VSCode里藏了部东方美学电影。**
**I hid an Oriental aesthetic movie inside my VSCode.**

## 🎨创作手记：从 ***"找主题"*** 到 ***"造主题"*** / 🎨 Creation Notes: From ***"Finding a Theme"*** to ***"Forging a Theme"***
**2021年的某个下午** ，我盯着VSCode主题市场🔎<em>翻了百来页</em>，<em>试了两天</em>。什么极简风、赛博朋克、莫兰迪色系......翻得我``眼睛发酸``——要么花里胡哨晃得代码都看不清的🙈，要么灰不溜秋像在看黑白电视的😭，``愣是没找到合眼缘的``。
**One afternoon in 2021**, I stared at the VSCode marketplace, scrolling through <em>hundreds of pages</em> for <em>two whole days</em>. Minimalism, Cyberpunk, Morandi... My ``eyes were getting sore``. They were either too flashy, making the code unreadable 🙈, or too dull, like watching black-and-white TV 😭. ``I just couldn't find "The One"``.

"找不到就自己造呗！自己动手丰衣足食"💪
"If I can't find it, I'll build it myself!" 💪

刚开始我还琢磨着从<em>世界名画里</em>找灵感🔎，盯着梵高的星空发了两小时呆——结果越看越慌😣：这要真做成主题，敲代码时怕不是要以为自己掉进油画颜料罐里了？直到某天整理电脑，突然翻出压箱底的 ***《青蛇》*** 电影截图——哎呦喂！``这不就是我心里想要的模样嘛``！😄
At first, I looked for inspiration in <em>world-famous paintings</em> 🔎. I stared at Van Gogh's Starry Night for two hours—but the more I looked, the more I panicked 😣: If this became a theme, I'd feel like I fell into a paint bucket while coding! Until one day, while organizing files, I stumbled upon screenshots from the movie *** "Green Snake" ***(1993). Aha! ``This is exactly what I wanted``! 😄

薄雾里的荷花池，张曼玉青蛇媚眼如丝的妖娆，王祖贤白蛇垂眸的温柔...每一帧都像浸在茶盏里的旧时光，是我刻在骨子里喜欢的<em>东方韵味</em>。我当场拍板："就它了！"
The lotus pool shrouded in mist, the enchanting charm of Maggie Cheung (Green Snake), the gentle gaze of Joey Wong (White Snake)... Every frame felt like old times steeped in a teacup. It was that <em>Oriental charm</em> engraved in my bones. I decided right then and there: "This is it!"

接下来就是疯狂抠颜色的日子。对着电影截图一帧帧放大，拿取色器点得鼠标都快磨出包浆——
Then came the days of crazy color picking. Zooming in frame by frame, clicking with the color picker until my mouse almost wore out—

- 背景深蓝得像西湖夜雾里的月光
- 青蛇衣摆的绿得是新抽的柳芽尖儿
- 白蛇头饰的红得像刚出窑的釉里红
- The Background Blue had to be like moonlight in the night mist of West Lake.
- The Green Snake Green had to match the tip of a newly sprouted willow bud.
- The Headpiece Red had to look like freshly kiln-fired glazed red porcelain.

光是调"青蛇绿"就试了二十多版，太亮显眼，太暗又没灵气，最后盯着屏幕揉了揉发酸的眼睛："就它！这才是最适合vscode的颜色～"
I tried over twenty versions just for that "Green Snake Green". Too bright? Distracting. Too dark? Lifeless. Finally, rubbing my sore eyes, I stared at the screen: "This is the one! The perfect color for VSCode."

配色搭好了，还得平衡好看和舒服——代码高亮不能晃眼，注释得清晰，光标得显眼但不能刺眼...前前后后折腾了几天，终于捣鼓出这个让我看了就想敲代码的主题。
Once the palette was set, I had to balance aesthetics with comfort—syntax highlighting shouldn't be glaring, comments must be clear, and the cursor needs to be visible but not piercing. After days of tweaking, I finally crafted this theme that makes me want to write code.

本来21年完成后就只自己用着乐呵，最近离职闲下来，突然想："这么好看的主题咋能藏着掖着呢？不如整理整理，给大家也添点代码里的东方浪漫～"
I originally made this in 2021 just for myself. Recently, having some free time, I thought: "Why hide such a beautiful theme? I should clean it up and share this bit of Oriental romance with everyone."

又调整了一些颜色，logo也画了（虽然手残党画了八版才满意），现在就等着把它挂出去分享给大家。
I adjusted a few more colors and drew a logo (struggled through 8 versions to get it right). Now, it's ready for you.

**敲代码是愉快的事，<a>愿你在这属于东方的美中敲出属于自己的精彩。</a>**
**Coding is a joy. <a>May you code your own brilliance amidst this Oriental beauty.</a>**

## 三步召唤青蛇主题（附施法口诀）/ Three Steps to Summon "Green Snake" (Magic Spell Included)

### ​Step1️⃣ 打开次元裂缝​​ / Open the Dimensional Rift

    - Ctrl+Shift+X 打开扩展商店（像召唤神龙一样帅又快） - Press Ctrl+Shift+X to open the Extensions Marketplace.

### ​Step2️⃣ 搜索关键词​​ / Chant the Spell

    - 输入 Green Snake Theme 或 青蛇（建议搭配敲键盘的BGM：电影原声《流光飞舞》） - Type "Green Snake Theme" or "青蛇" (Recommended BGM: Movie Soundtrack "Dancing Lights").

### ​Step3️⃣ 点击传送阵​​ / Click the Portal

    - 给主题点个⭐ 点击「Install」按钮（安装时会有青烟特效在脑子哦） - Click the "Install" button. It will show a smoke effect in your mind while installing.

​​<h3>⚠️ 重要提示 / Important Notice​​</h3>

若出现「找不到主题」请检查：
If you can't find the theme, please check:

- 是否在​​扩展商店​​搜索（不是浏览器！）
- Are you searching in the Extension Marketplace (not the browser)?

- 网络是否畅通（没网=没路，主题没法到你这）
- Is your network working? (No internet = no path, the theme can't reach you.)

- 尝试重启VSCode（给程序一点施法时间）
- Restart VSCode (Give the program a few casting seconds.)
